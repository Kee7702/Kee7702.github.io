About PanoramaSwitcher 1.06
PanoramaSwitcher couldn't be a more self-explanatory title, but I'll tell you anyways: Inside you'll find mostly every panorama ever in Minecraft, all accessible to you in one resource pack for you to look back on at any time.
Panoramas for Bedrock, Java, or Education Edition
Panoramas for Legacy Console Edition
Release Notes - Spring 2025
Added 8 new panoramas: Holiday 2017 (bedrock), Poisonous Potato Update (java), Tricky Trials, The Garden Awakens, and more
Downloads
Download a .mcpack or .zip file, import the pack into Minecraft, then apply the pack. Afterwards, you'll finally be able to enjoy a variety of interesting panoramas by tapping the active pack in settings, and clicking the cog or wrench (depending on game version) icon.
