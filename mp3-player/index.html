About MP3 Player
MP3 Player is a simple music selector for LittleBigPlanet 3. It contains all base-game music tracks with capabilities to add any additional tracks you'd like.
Release Notes - 2026
After a long time, I've been hard at work refining MP3 Player to be as efficient yet capable as it can be.
