About NBT Preset Editor
NBT Preset Editor was a concept from February 2023, made during a time where Toolbox for Minecraft was a more substantial part of my life, and it was built to showcase a potential solution to the problem of editing NBT data. It works by having set edit points within a large block of JSON. Although this concept was brought back from the dead, I do not plan to expand on this. Mostly because modded clients for Minecraft Bedrock are effectively gone, but I've also never been good at creating NBTs.
